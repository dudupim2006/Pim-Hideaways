Casa da Pintora

Olhão, Portugal

Situated in the heart of Olhão, this century-old townhouse carries the soul of a place that has lived multiple lives. Once an abandoned laundry room with stone arcades, it was carefully restored by Dutch artist Meinke Flesseman. The original tiles were lovingly preserved, and those that couldn't be saved were remade by hand by the artist herself.

The Grand Dining Room

Featuring massive white arches that hint at the building's history as a historic laundry room, the open-plan dining space has been beautifully restored. It serves as the perfect central hub for large gatherings and shared meals.

The Hand-Painted Kitchen

A culinary space adorned with striking blue and white Azulejo tiles. The original pieces from the century-old building were carefully preserved, while missing sections were lovingly hand-crafted and painted by the artist Meinke Flesseman.

Rooftop Terrace

Ascend to the sunny rooftop terrace, offering panoramic views of Olhão's famous white cubic houses. Complete with cozy sun loungers, an outdoor dining setup, and spectacular golden-hour views over the Ria Formosa.

Courtyard & Plunge Pool

A private inner courtyard providing a secluded oasis right in the center of the historical town. The minimalist plunge pool and surrounding sun deck offer a refreshing escape during the warm Algarve afternoons.

Restful Suites

Comfortable bedrooms offering peace and quiet after a day of exploring. The interior design blends carefully sourced antique furniture, modern wooden paneling, and warm ambient lighting to create a deeply relaxing atmosphere.
