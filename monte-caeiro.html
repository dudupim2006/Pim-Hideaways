Monte Caeiro

Porto Covo, Portugal

Monte Caeiro offers a country house that provides our guests with the perfect harmony between the natural landscapes of Alentejo and the most beautiful beaches of the Portuguese coast. The house is located between the center of Porto Covo and Ribeira da Azenha. Just a short walk from Monte Caeiro, you can enjoy the beaches of Ilha do Pessegueiro and Praia do Queimado. Also near the property, you can explore other beaches such as Praia do Malhão and Praia dos Aivados (a 10-minute drive).

Suite I

Bedroom with a double bed, private bathroom, and shutter doors with access to the outdoor patio. The room extends to an outdoor patio featuring sofas, side tables, and hammocks swinging between the shade and the sunny porch.

Suite II

Bedroom with 2 single beds, which can be joined to form a double bed. Private bathroom and exit to the outdoor patio equipped with sofas, side tables, and hammocks.

Suite III

Bedroom with a double bed, private bathroom, and a small living room with a single sofa bed. Access to the outdoor patio with hammocks and a view of the pool.

Mezzanine

Mezzanine living room with two single beds plus a sofa bed. TV, DVD, and reading area. Ideal for children and teenagers! Access to the terrace overlooking the forest and pool, inviting you to watch the sunrise or sunset. A space where you can have your first coffee of the day or an evening cocktail after the beach!

Exterior Bedroom

Bedroom with a triple bunk bed (double bed + single bed), private bathroom, and an outdoor living room with a fireplace. This room is independent from the main house and is located in front of the pool.

Activities & Surroundings

At Monte Caeiro, you are invited to explore our own private hiking trail, various picnic spots, the small forest, the outdoor fireplace for starry nights, and much more! Surrounding the property, you can also explore the Rota Vicentina trail, or the coastal hiking path to Porto Covo (Fisherman's Trail).
